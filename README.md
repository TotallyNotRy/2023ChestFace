
# 2023ChestFace
Built off of Dev9998's ancient mod LegacyChestFace, patched and fixed to work today.
<img width="683" height="682" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2b71bb81-69a4-4786-99f2-02a482f33e21" />
## Compatibility
Should be compatible with pretty much everything except any other mods that change VRRig's textures/materials.
AntiVMTworm included.
## Source Texture
The textures used in this mod are DIRECTLY ripped from the Holiday 2023 update, released on December 1st 2023.
## Known Issues
When wearing the face from the Transformable Werewolf bundle of 2026, face may appear blank white or clamped to the game's atlas of speaking mouths. Weird glitch, but its pretty rare.
